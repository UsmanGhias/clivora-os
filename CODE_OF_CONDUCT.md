# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CLIVORA is a global community for people building better tools for freelancers and clients. We commit to a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free environment regardless of background, identity, experience, nationality, language, disability, religion, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting correction.
- Protect private and security-sensitive information.
- Give credit and respect licences.
- Welcome beginners without lowering quality or safety standards.
- Keep product, issue, and review discussions relevant.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, hate speech, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, impersonation, deliberate misinformation, spam, fake contribution activity, plagiarism, credential sharing, security exploitation, and repeated disruption are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **contact@clivora.io** with subject `[CONDUCT] CLIVORA`. The project lead may edit or remove content, close contributions, limit participation, or ban accounts when necessary to protect the community.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, reviews, issues, discussions, community spaces, events, and public representation of the project.
